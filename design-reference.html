// 09 · Pointer

Custom cursor

A small square reticle replaces the native pointer (fine pointers only), leaving a fading teal afterimage trail along its path. It expands and rotates over interactive elements and contracts on click. Built from .cursor + .trace and a small mousemove script that injects itself — no markup required. Auto-disabled on touch devices and under reduced-motion.

Live on this page

Move your pointer to see the reticle track and the teal squares trace behind it. Hover a button or link to watch the reticle open and rotate; press to contract it.
